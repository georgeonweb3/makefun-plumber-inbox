Build the make.fun entry "The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email" (u-ravendell-the-plumber-s-inbox-a-120-word-cold-email-9ab3e51c).
Write main.py (Python, deterministic, no network) that writes one cold
email of about 120 words, subject line included, to /output/entry.txt,
pitching a bookkeeping service to a busy plumber. It must show it knows a
plumber's week (early starts, emergency call-outs, paperwork at night) in
the first lines, make the offer small and concrete with a plain price and
first step, end with one easy ask, and read like a real person's email:
plain subject, a name, no hype, no fake familiarity.
